# HR-Data-Analysis-Dashboard (Interactive Dashboard Creation using Microsoft Power BI)
## Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze employee attrition and identify the factors affecting employee turnover, job satisfaction, salary, age, education, job role, and employee experience.

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/Shrikant-pawar26/Data-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/HR_Analytics.csv">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the total number of employees.
- What is the total employee attrition.
- What is the overall attrition rate.
- What is the average employee age.
- What is the average salary.
- What is the average number of years employees stay at the company. 
- What is the attrition by education.
- What is the attrition by age group. 
- What is the attrition by salary slab. 
- What is the attrition by years at company. 
-  job role has the highest attrition.
- What is the job satisfaction level based on job role. 
- How does attrition vary by gender.

## process
- Imported the HR dataset into Power BI.
- Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.
- Checked data types and data consistency.
- Created calculated measures using DAX.
- Created KPIs for employee count, attrition, attrition rate, average age, salary, and years at company.
- Created charts to analyze employee attrition.
- Designed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard.
- Used Power BI filters and visualizations for data exploration.

## Project Insights
- 133 employees have left the organization.
- Overall attrition rate is 13.8%.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition with 62 employees.
- Research Scientists have the second-highest attrition with 47 employees.
- Employees with salaries up to 5K have the highest attrition.
- The 26–35 age group has the highest attrition.
- Attrition is higher among male employees in the dashboard.
- Attrition is concentrated among employees with relatively fewer years at the company.
- Job satisfaction varies across different job roles.

## Final Conclusion

The analysis shows that employee attrition is mainly concentrated among 26–35-year-old employees, lower salary groups, and Laboratory Technicians. The company can focus on career growth, salary improvement, employee engagement, and job satisfaction to improve employee retention and reduce attrition.
